# Observation-only traditional four-route baseline

This is a strong traditional control under the same observable-input restriction, not a proposed learned contribution. The existing color prototype is learned from TRAIN positive endpoints without new hyperparameter tuning. It supplies one RGB-D surface endpoint from the exact instruction. Workspace bounds are the TRAIN path minimum/maximum plus a fixed 6 cm margin. No DEV goal, complete obstacle geometry, route type, future path, or acceptance label enters generation.

Current RGB-D is projected into a fixed 2.5 cm voxel grid. A cell-center ray is considered visibly free when it is in front of its observed depth by 2 cm; other observed surface voxels are inflated by 2 cm plus half the voxel diagonal. **This is a discrete free-space approximation, not a continuous physical 2 cm clearance certificate.** Surface quantization and segment-to-grid error are not jointly bounded, and visibility is tested at cell centers. The independent, unchanged complete tip-segment/true-box evaluator can therefore reject a grid-clear path.

Space behind the observed depth or outside the view is unknown and blocked. Two explicit local allowances are recorded: the current measured tip's 4 cm sphere, and the predicted target component within a TRAIN-derived radius (half the prototype's observed extent plus one voxel) and a 3 cm depth shell. Target contact never removes observed points from another component. Each record preserves allowance dimensions, exact voxel indices, number of newly free and unknown cells, examined permission-touching edges, and route edges that use the allowance. No DEV failure is used to enlarge either region.

Exactly four sequential A* calls produce the K=4 output slots. Each call is capped at 20,000 node expansions or 2 seconds. The graph has 26 neighbor directions, and every voxel touched at a diagonal corner/edge must be free, so diagonal endpoint checks alone cannot permit corner cutting. Previously used edges receive a fixed cost multiplier of `1 + 4 * previous_use_count`. Graph expansion and edge checks are search operations, not additional complete trajectory candidates; both are counted separately. A failed or exhausted search retains a NaN slot. There is no repeated-seed search, filtering a larger pool, or repair.

Raw complete voxel routes include exact measured-current and predicted-endpoint connectors. They are saved alongside fixed arc-length H24 outputs. Raw and H24 complete line segments are checked against the grid proxy with conservative voxel supercover, and resampling changes are recorded. A failed connector or H24 check does not trigger replacement or removal. Only after every candidate has been saved does the existing evaluator read DEV target/box truth. Its box-only `TipValid` remains distinct from unverified full-arm, other-body, IK and execution validity.

The first real run will use the lexicographically first TRAIN observation solely to measure bounded cost and verify the execution path, then all four predeclared DEV parents and all 12 target instructions. Parameters above are fixed before either run. The CPU self-test has passed for two passages, edge penalties, blocked diagonal corners, full segment voxel traversal, H24 endpoint preservation, failed search and depth-behind-surface unknown blocking. A preliminary import-path failure was corrected by loading historical sibling evaluator modules from the fixed source only after predictions are saved; it did not alter geometry or scores.

Implementation: `scripts/observation_multiroute_astar.py`. Run `--self-test` for the pure geometry check; `--data ... --output ... --train-benchmark-only` for the first-TRAIN cost probe; omit the last flag for complete DEV evaluation. Real evidence and timing will be appended after immutable-source CPU runs.
